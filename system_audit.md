# FinalFrame: The Complete System Map
*Professional Operational Guide: v0.6.4*

This document serves as the guide for FinalFrame. It details the workflow from starting a project to the final export and distribution of high-quality video content.

---

## 1. The Entrance: Authentication & Getting Started
**Think of this as: Starting Your Session.**

### Step 1.1: The Landing Page
When you arrive at `finalframe.ai`, the interface displays the **Studio_Intelligence_Active** signal. Our core mission: **Simplified video production that honors your creative rules.**
- **The Get_Started Button:** The primary entry point. Clicking this begins your journey.
- **Video_Showcases:** View real-time examples of videos created in our studio.

### Step 1.2: Account Setup & Studio Profile
Before you can start creating, the system needs to set up your **Studio_Profile** and establish your **Creative DNA**.
- **Secure_Login:** Simple entrance via authenticated email.
- **Onboarding Guide:** A 9-step setup where you define your brand's style (e.g., "High Energy" vs. "Minimalist").
- **Studio_Setup:** Once finished, you are given a **Master_Workspace**. This is your private command center.

---

## 2. Project_Hub: Creating Your Vision
**Think of this as: Setting Up Your Mission.**

When you select **"Create_Project"**, the system launches the Project Wizard. This is a **Zero_Guesswork_Zone**.

- **Step 1 (The Identity):** Give your project a **Name** and select a **Production_Type** (e.g., "Commercial" for high-impact ads, or "Product Demo" for showing off software).
- **Step 2 (The Vision):** Describe your **Creative_Intent**. Tell the system exactly what you want.
- **Step 3 (The Ingredients):** Upload **Reference_Assets**. Add product photos, logos, or styles. These are held safely until you're ready.
- **Step 4 (The DNA):** The system applies your **Studio_Profile** settings. You can make adjustments for this specific project.
- **Step 5 (Submission):** Review your choices. Clicking **"Create Project"** saves the plan and starts the process.

---

## 3. AI_Director: The Video Blueprint
**Think of this as: Your Production Plan.**

Once the project is created, you land in the **Blueprint Editor**. This is your **Drafting Stage**.

### What is a Video_Blueprint?
A Video_Blueprint is a collection of **Scenes**. Each scene has:
- **Scene_Goal:** Why this scene exists.
- **Camera_Movement:** Professional controls like pan, tilt, and zoom.
- **Asset_Link:** A direct connection between a scene and one of your **Reference_Assets**.

### Controls:
- **"Generate_Blueprint":** Ask the AI Director to build the plan based on your vision.
- **Reorder & Edit:** Drag scenes to change the order or refine the text to match your style.
- **"Approve_&_Authorize":** The **Final Check**. The system makes sure you have:
  1. A primary logo.
  2. At least one visual asset.
  3. Enough scenes for a full video.
  
If everything is ready, you move to production!

---

## 4. Video_Pipeline: Producing the Magic
**Think of this as: High-Quality Video Production.**

When the project is `approved`, the **Video_Pipeline** starts the creation process.

1. **The Lock:** The system secures the project so it creates one version at a time.
2. **Production_Style:** The system picks a "Recipe" (High-Quality Model). 
   - *Example:* "Commercial" results in the **CINEMATIC_REALISM** style. 
   - *Rule:* We prioritize visual results over technical model names.
3. **Asset Enforcement:** If you linked an image to a scene, the system *must* use it. It won't guess what the background should be.
4. **Rendering:** The engine builds each 5-second scene with high detail.
5. **Quality Check:** Once finished, the video is saved and the status changes to `rendered`.

---

## 5. Project_Dashboard: Review & Remix
**Think of this as: Refinement & Polishing.**

Once your video is ready, you can see it on your dashboard. Now you can make changes.

### The Remix Hub:
Use the sidebar to make adjustments in real-time.
- You can say: *"Zoom in more on Scene 2"* or *"Change the background music."*
- The system updates the plan and re-renders the specific scenes instantly.

### Status Indicators:
- **Rendered:** Your video is ready for review.
- **Rendering:** The system is currently focused on producing your video.
- **Archived:** Move finished projects to storage to keep your workspace clean.

---

## 6. Final_Export: Delivery & Sharing
**Think of this as: Ready for the World.**

Once you're happy, start the **Final_Export** process.
- **Format Choices:** Select Cinema (16:9) or Mobile (9:16).
- **Integrity Check:** The system does a final scan to ensure everything meets professional standards.
- **Download:** Get your high-quality video file or share a private link with others.

---

## Summary Checklist:
1. **[Entrance]** Click "Get Started."
2. **[Setup]** Set up your Studio Profile.
3. **[Create]** Click " + New Project."
4. **[Plan]** Enter your Vision -> Upload Assets -> Click "Create."
5. **[Review]** Click "Generate_Blueprint" -> Refine if needed -> Click "Approve."
6. **[Production]** Wait for "Rendering" to finish.
7. **[Export]** Review your video -> Remix if needed -> Click "Final_Export."

**Congratulations! Your video is ready for production.**
