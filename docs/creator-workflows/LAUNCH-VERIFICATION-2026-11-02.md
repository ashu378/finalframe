## Remaining full-launch blockers

1. **Long-form movies and whole-movie delivery:** `convex/productions.ts` still limits approved episode plans to **60 seconds / 200 shots**; episode management uses bounded short episodes. No thirty-minute movie, cross-episode final movie merge, long-form cost/performance/load or crash-resume acceptance was completed. Removing limits alone is not an implementation.
2. **Captions and advanced transitions:** authoring-to-canonical-export support needs implementation and genuine rendered visual acceptance. Current code rejects unsupported requests rather than pretending to render them.
3. **Automatic music analysis:** known-grid cuts work; automatic beat detection is absent/unverified. Generative music and SFX remain distinct unverified routes.
4. **Personal voices and human performance:** real-consented voice cloning, real-person hairstyle/appearance continuity, separate lip sync, performance transfer, accepted-job extension and upscaling require their actual supported routes and independent tests. Reference voice synthesis currently fails closed because its hard output-duration bound is not certified; no consented owner recording was supplied for a live clone test.
5. **Creative quality across full productions:** exact Pidgin pronunciation/dialogue, recurring human characters, clothing/product/brand continuity, multi-day story development, every target language and full campaign/movie quality require owner review and broader acceptance. The two-scene fictional character test establishes only its specific retained references.
6. **Complete end-user automation:** some acceptance compositions use manually authored canonical plans and test drivers. That verifies the backend production path, not that every creator can type one idea and receive every complex finished format without further UI/configuration work. Full prompt-to-finished tests must be recorded for those variants.
7. **Production rollout and operational scale:** these are Preview/development results. Production configuration, release migration/backups, sustained load/memory/callback outage recovery, monitoring, and owner creative acceptance remain release gates. Billing/email remain deferred by the owner, not silently enabled.

Because the owner requires launching the whole promised product together, these blockers must be completed and verified before claiming the whole-product launch is ready. Buying more OpenRouter balance does not implement the missing stages.
