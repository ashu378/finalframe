# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Creators, first-time video makers, agencies and clients managing independent video projects.

## Product Purpose
Turn an idea, script or uploaded media into a project-owned production, with a reviewed plan, approved cost, scenes, takes, editing and downloads.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Keep the existing Next.js, Convex, OpenRouter and renderer architecture. Preserve project isolation, approved snapshots, permissions and deliberate spending approvals. Never change Yafpay. Redesign does not activate email or payments and does not purchase AI media.

## Brand Commitments
FinalFrame. Owner approved black, white and lime on15November2026, using the supplied images and Higgsfield as visual inspiration. Plain language and ease of use take priority. Interactive 3D and mobile touch motion belong in the public showcase; editing controls remain stable.

## Evidence on Hand
Retained reviewed fictional fixtures, production screenshots and exports are documented in the existing release checklist. Technical fixture success does not establish every creative claim.

## Product Principles
- Each project owns its cast, voice and media.
- Creators review before an expensive action.
- Beginners see a clear next step; professionals retain detailed controls.
- Existing work and downloadable history remain intact.

## Plain-language interface
Owner instruction16November: use everyday language throughout. Keep scenes, shots and takes distinct; explain necessary technical details next to the control or behind an optional disclosure. All screen groups are being redesigned; full end-to-end acceptance follows email/recovery and payments.
